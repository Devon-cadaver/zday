# Disaster Radio: voice-over script

Record it clean (no effects): the game adds the old radio crackle and the tinny speaker sound.
Send me one MP3 (or MP4) with about 2 seconds of silence between lines, or one file per line named by its number.

## Finding the voice in ElevenLabs

An Orson Welles clone isn't an option (ElevenLabs only clones a voice with that person's consent), but you can get the same feel with an original voice:

- **Voice Library**: search "old-time radio", "1940s announcer", "newsreel", "mid-Atlantic" or "vintage narrator".
- **Voice Design** (make a voice from a description). Paste something like:
  > A deep, resonant baritone male voice, a 1940s American radio news announcer with a mid-Atlantic accent, crisp theatrical diction, commanding and dramatic, slightly fast-paced.

## Getting emotion

- **Model**: use **Eleven v3** if you have it. It understands tags in square brackets, such as `[urgent]`, `[shouting]`, `[nervous]`, `[whispers]`, `[gasps]`, `[excited]`, `[serious]`, `[relieved]` and `[sighs]`. The script below already has them; delete them if your model reads them out loud.
- **Settings**:
  - Stability: lower, about 30–40% (more expressive, less flat).
  - Style exaggeration: about 40–60%.
  - Speaker boost: on.
- **Writing**:
  - Ellipses (...) make hesitations.
  - Exclamation marks raise the energy.
  - Short sentences hit harder.
- **Takes**: make each line on its own and regenerate 2–3 times. The takes vary a lot, so keep the best one.

## The script (don't read the numbers)

1. [calm] You're listening to K-D-I-S, Disaster Radio. Stay tuned... and stay safe.
2. [nervous] We're getting reports of people... biting people. [urgent] Lock your doors and stay off the streets!
3. [urgent] Tornado on the ground! [shouting] I repeat, a tornado has touched down! Get to a basement... now!
4. [shouting] Earthquake! Drop, cover, and hold on! Stay away from windows!
5. [urgent] Flash flood warning! The water is rising fast. Get to higher ground!
6. [urgent] Tsunami warning! The sea is pulling back from the shore. [shouting] Get to the top floor of a tall building, now!
7. [serious] A riot has broken out downtown. Avoid the area. Police are moving in.
8. [serious] Ladies and gentlemen, we interrupt this broadcast. [nervous] Strange lights are falling over the city... something is landing. [gasps] It's... it's not from this world!
9. [excited] Look to the sky! A meteor is heading straight for the city. [shouting] Take cover!
10. [urgent] The ground is giving way! A sinkhole is swallowing the street. Stay back!
11. [shouting] The mountain has erupted! Lava and ash are heading our way. Evacuate immediately!
12. [urgent] A wall of dust is rolling in from the desert. Get inside, shut your windows, and cover your face!
13. [serious] An arctic blast is sweeping in. Temperatures are crashing. Stay indoors and keep warm!
14. [nervous] A massive solar flare is hitting us now... power is failing all over the city... [whispers] if you can hear this... stay off the roads!
15. [serious] This is not a drill. A missile is inbound. [shouting] Take shelter immediately! This is not a drill!
16. [urgent] Alarms are sounding at the power plant. There's been a leak. Stay indoors and close all windows.
17. [nervous] Scientists are... baffled. Something is pulling the city apart from the inside. [shouting] Hold on to something!
18. [excited] Super villains spotted over the city! Stay inside and wait for the heroes!
19. [nervous] Something huge is moving under the streets... the ground is rippling! [urgent] Get off the road!
20. [shouting] A giant monster has come ashore, and it's heading for downtown. Run!
21. [gasps] Wait... is that...? [excited] Yes! The heroes are here!
22. [relieved] The worst is over. Rescue teams are on the scene. If you need help, stay where you are. They're coming.
23. [calm] All clear. Repeat, all clear. Crews are moving in to clear the streets and start rebuilding.
24. [relieved] It's over, folks. [sighs] Take a breath. The rescue teams are on site, and help is on the way.
