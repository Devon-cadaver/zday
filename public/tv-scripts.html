Each picture is from Watch itself, taken at that point of the fight to show which enemy appears. For the pictures the camera was handed back to the game for a moment and the blur turned off, so the enemy is clear; on TV the same moment is filmed from the helicopters. How each town goes
  1. WHAT HITS THE TOWN? Bet on three disasters at their odds; the one that comes is drawn at those odds.
  2. Then a second slip: the hero wins or the disaster wins, and under or over a line of buildings down.
  3. One of your heroes plays it by computer, with one life. The result is drawn at the odds you saw and the fight is steered to it.
  4. The result card, then the next town.
