# My Little Disaster

**Save it, wreck it, or sit back and bet on it!**

A tiny tilt-shift city full of little people going about their day — until a tornado touches down, a kaiju wades in from the sea, or the saucers arrive. Run the rescue, become the disaster, mess about in the Sandbox, or watch the end of the world live on Disaster TV.

Plays in your browser on phone, tablet or PC. No download, no account.

---

## Four ways to play

### 🚨 Command — save the town
You're the emergency commander. Each 10-minute round throws two to five incidents at the town: zombie outbreaks, earthquakes, riots, floods, fires, alien landings, super villains and worse as you level up. Send in the police, evacuation buses, fire trucks, water bombers and rescue helicopters, the army, an airstrike... or, if it really comes to it, the nuke. Clear every incident and the town is safe.

- 12 levels, new units and incidents unlocked as you go, upgrades to buy
- A road of 17 towns, from a little mountain village to the big city and the neon towns of the Far Isles

### 🌪️ Control — be the disaster
Pick a disaster and *drive* it for three minutes. Steer the tornado through downtown, guide the giant worm under the streets, stomp the kaiju through the harbour, beam people up from a flying saucer, start the riot, spread the outbreak. Every disaster has its own controls. Score, ranks and a best score for each one.

### 🧪 Sandbox — anything goes
Every disaster, any time, anywhere: tornado, earthquake, flood, tsunami, riot, alien invasion, meteor, sinkhole, volcano, dust storm, cold wave, solar flare, nuclear attack, plant meltdown, black hole, super villains, giant worm, kaiju, killer plant, dimensional rift — and zombies. Change the weather (9 kinds), travel to any town you've won, and then get stuck in yourself:

- **Fly a plane** — stunt plane, F-16, A-10 Warthog, cloud seeder, B-2 and B-52. Seed the storm clouds, drop bombs, napalm, a carpet run or a nuke. Chase or cockpit view. The disasters fight back: tornadoes suck you in, the kaiju throws cars at you.
- **Be a hero** — fly as Nova, Dragonfly or Tempo. Lock on to a villain and you're on them in a second: tap the glowing weak spots, then **Spin & Toss** them across town, or hit **Random Move** for a flying kick, a laser blast, a drag down the street by the neck, or the **Sky Slam** — hold to launch them into the sky, tap to fly up after them, hold to smash them back into the ground. The harder the hit, the bigger the crater.
- **On the ground** — drive a tank, or patrol as a police officer with a six-shooter that fires by itself. Jump in and out of the police car, fire truck and ambulance. Hold back the zombies.
- **Take over a disaster** — pick a flying saucer, an alien walker, the giant worm, the kaiju or a tornado and control it yourself.
- **Play the band** — the town's punk band is always playing somewhere. Tap the stage, hit **Control the band**, and tap the balls to the beat of the theme song. Build a combo for flame jets and fireworks.

### 📺 Watch — Disaster TV
The end of the world, live. Sit back as disaster after disaster hits town after town, filmed from news helicopters and rooftop cameras (which don't always survive). Call what hits next — get it right and help arrives early, and you score points.

## A city that's alive
- Thousands of little people who walk, drive, panic, film on their phones, hide indoors, climb onto roofs and get rescued
- Traffic, fires that spread, day and night, weather, buildings that crack, burn and come down
- Super heroes and super villains who fight it out above the streets
- A punk band and a goth-metal girl band playing the two theme songs live, with fireworks
- Original music

## Controls
**Phone / tablet:** drag to look around, pinch to zoom, tap to act. In planes and as a hero, drag anywhere to steer. On the ground, press anywhere for a joystick.
**PC:** drag with the mouse, wheel to zoom, click to act. Planes and heroes: arrows / WASD to steer, Shift and Ctrl for speed, Space to drop or lock on, Q to change payload, V to switch view, I to invert, Esc to land.

Tap the 🎵 button for the music.

## Notes
- Your progress is saved in your browser on this device.
- Cartoon destruction: tiny figures run, panic and get rescued. No blood or gore.
- On phones and tablets the game uses a lighter detail level on its own, so it runs smoothly there too.
